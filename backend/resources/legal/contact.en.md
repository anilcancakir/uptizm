One person runs this service, so a message here reaches the operator directly rather than a
queue. There is no promised response time and this page will not invent one.

## Email

Write to [[legal.contact_email]] for anything: a question about a plan, a monitor behaving in
a way the documentation does not explain, a security report, a bug, or an invoice.

The form below, when it is offered, sends to that same address and nowhere else. It exists so
you do not have to leave the page, not to route you into a ticket system that does not exist.

## What helps

A monitor that is misbehaving is much easier to answer with its name, the region the failing
check ran from, and roughly when you noticed. A billing question is easier with the address
the account is registered under.

Please do not include passwords, API tokens or the credentials a monitor uses to authenticate.
Nothing here needs them, and email is the wrong place for them.

## Data protection requests

Access, correction, deletion and the rest of your rights go to [[legal.rights_email]]. Say
which right you are exercising and which account or address it concerns, and the Privacy page
explains what happens next and how long it may take.

## Security reports

A vulnerability report goes to [[legal.contact_email]] as well. Please describe what you found
and how to reproduce it, and please do not test against another customer's data or against a
status page you do not own.
